        *   Fetch all journals where `sourceNo` is null.

        *   Update the journal record with the fetched `sourceNo`.
    *   Execute this script to clean/backfill historical data.

    *   **Update Creation Logic**: Modify `journals.service.ts` -> `createJournal` to fetch and store `sourceNo` at the time of creation.

*   **`prisma/schema.prisma`**: Add field and index.
